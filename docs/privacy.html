Privacy

Effective 21 September 2026

  • No account, no ads, no analytics. The app itself tracks nothing.
  • What you write stays on your phone unless you allow Claude to read it. The app asks first.
  • If you allow it, what you write is sent to Anthropic's Claude to choose passages. This app keeps no copy.
  • Videos play through YouTube, and podcasts stream from each show's host, only when you open or play them.

What stays on your phone

  • What you type, unless you allow Claude to read it.
  • Passages you keep. They are stored only on your device, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • Bible translations you download, and your choice about Claude.

When you allow Claude to read what you write

The app asks before anything is sent. If you allow it:

  • What you write, and any feelings you select, are sent over an encrypted connection to the app's reading service, which runs on Cloudflare, and from there to Anthropic's API, so that Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, can choose passages for you and say why.
  • If you open How to live this on a passage, the same text is sent again with that passage's reference, so Claude can write how it applies to you.
  • The reading service does not store or log what you write.
  • Anthropic handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

You can change your mind at any time in the app under About → Claude's reading. With Claude off, passages are matched on your phone and nothing you write is sent.

Reports

If you report something Claude wrote, the app sends that writing and the reason you choose. It does not send what you wrote. Reports are kept for up to 90 days so they can be reviewed, and are then deleted automatically.

Watch and Listen

  • Browsing the list of films and videos loads nothing from YouTube or Google. There are no thumbnails, for exactly that reason.
  • Opening a video loads YouTube's player in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). From that point Google receives information such as your IP address and device details, and more as you watch, and handles it under Google's privacy policy. Privacy-enhanced mode limits what YouTube does with it; it does not remove Google from the picture.
  • Podcast episode lists are fetched through the app's reading service, so podcast hosts do not see you browse. The service handles your IP address as described under Other network requests, and keeps no record of which shows you look at.
  • When you play an episode, the audio streams directly from that show's own host, which receives your IP address and device details under its own policy.

Other network requests

  • Downloading a Bible translation fetches a file from GitHub. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Requests to the reading service carry your IP address. It is used for a short time to limit how many requests one device can make, and is not written to storage by the app. Cloudflare, which runs the service, keeps short-lived operational logs as part of providing it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
