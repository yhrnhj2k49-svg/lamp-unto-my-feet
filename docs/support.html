Answers to common questions

How do I get Claude's reading?

The first time you search, the app asks whether Claude may read what you write. If you chose to keep it on your phone and want to change that, go to About → Claude's reading.

I got passages, but not "Read by Claude"

Either Claude could not be reached, or Claude's reading is turned off. The passages you see were matched on your phone instead, from 167 passages chosen for the moments they speak to. Try again when you have a connection.

Where are my kept passages?

In the Kept tab, on your phone only. There is no account and no backup, so deleting the app deletes them.

Which Bible translations can I read?

Ten complete translations, free to download from the Bible tab: Berean Standard, New Heart English, King James, American King James, American Standard, Young's Literal, Darby, Bible in Basic English, Catholic Public Domain, and Webster's. Once downloaded they work with no signal.

NIV, ESV, NLT, NASB, CSB and The Message are copyrighted and cannot be included. Every chapter has links that open those on Bible Gateway instead.

Something Claude wrote was wrong or harmful

Tap Report this reading below a reading, or Report under a passage's How to live this. Only what Claude wrote is sent, never your own words.

Is a passage quoted correctly?

Every passage is the King James Version, checked word for word against the complete King James text. The plain reading beside each one is a paraphrase written for the app, not a translation; where they differ, the scripture is what counts.

If you are in danger

This app is not a substitute for someone who can be with you right now.

  • US and Canada: call or text 988
  • UK and Ireland: call 116 123 (Samaritans)
  • Anywhere: findahelpline.com
  • If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
